# 💍 Royal Islamic Wedding Invitation

> **In the Name of Allah, The Most Beneficent, The Most Merciful**  
> *"Oh Allah guide this marriage to the best of understanding, happiness, prosperity & success — Ameen."*

An interactive luxury web invitation for the wedding ceremony of **Mohammed Sinan** with **Aysha Zohara** on **Thursday, 31st December 2026** at **Unity Hall, Thokkottu Mangalore**.

Hosted with honor by **Mrs. & Mr. Mohammed Hussain Beary**.

---

## ✨ Features & Interactive Flow

1. **Royal Envelope Welcome Modal**:
   - 3D Wax Seal with golden embossed floral emblem
   - URL personalization support (`?guest=GuestName` or `?g=GuestName`)
   - Interactive envelope opening experience with golden confetti & velvet rose petals burst
2. **Atmospheric Background & Music**:
   - Floating Islamic geometric motifs & falling rose petals
   - Ambient wedding nasheed audio with animated soundwave player, play/pause & mute controls
3. **Mughal / Islamic Palace Courtyard Aesthetics**:
   - Chandelier lighting, palace courtyard, and warm golden filigree borders
   - Arabic Bismillah calligraphy & Surah Ar-Rum (30:21)
4. **Physical Card Poetry & Compliments**:
   - Left-flap heirloom poem: *"A Day of togetherness, a moment of joy and emotions, a dream of every parent today a reality of us"*
   - *"With best compliments from: Relatives & Friends"*
   - *"Your presence & dua is our greatest gift"*
   - Family residence details in Mumbai
5. **Live Countdown Timer**:
   - Live countdown to Thursday, 31st December 2026 (Maghrib time)
6. **Program Timeline**:
   - Maghrib Namaz, Nikah Solemnization, Congratulatory Duas, Celebratory Dinner till 10:00 PM
7. **Direct Navigation & Calendar Sync**:
   - One-click Google Maps directions to Unity Hall, Thokkottu Mangalore
   - One-click Add to Google Calendar & Apple / Outlook iCal (.ics download)
8. **WhatsApp RSVP Integration**:
   - Interactive attendance selection (Attending / Family attendance) with guest count
   - Direct WhatsApp message generation
9. **Dua Wishes Wall**:
   - Sunnah wedding dua with Arabic, transliteration, and translation
   - Interactive guestbook to post heartfelt prayers and congratulations
10. **Multimedia Modals**:
    - High-definition animated video invitation reel player
    - Original printed invitation card lightbox viewer
